# 🚦 CivicFix

### AI-Powered Urban Issue Detection, Prioritization & Resolution Platform

> **Report smarter. Prioritize faster. Resolve better.**

---

## 📌 Overview

**CivicFix** is an AI-powered civic issue management platform that connects **citizens, municipal authorities, and field workers** to improve the reporting, prioritization, verification, and resolution of urban problems.

It enables citizens to report urban issues such as:

* 🕳️ Potholes
* 🛣️ Damaged roads
* 💡 Broken streetlights
* 🗑️ Overflowing garbage bins
* 🌳 Fallen or damaged trees
* 🚧 Other civic infrastructure issues

CivicFix uses **AI to detect issues, identify duplicate complaints, estimate severity, prioritize urgent problems, recommend suitable actions, and support verification**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Urban civic issues are often reported through disconnected channels, making it difficult for authorities to:

* Identify genuine and urgent problems
* Handle duplicate complaints
* Prioritize issues effectively
* Assign issues to appropriate field workers
* Track issue resolution
* Maintain transparency with citizens

### 💡 Our Approach

CivicFix provides a centralized platform where civic issues can move through a structured workflow:

**Report → Detect → Verify → Prioritize → Assign → Resolve → Track**

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Issue Detection

Uses AI to analyze reported civic issues and identify the type and characteristics of the problem.

### 🔄 Duplicate Complaint Detection

Identifies potentially duplicate complaints so that the same civic issue is not processed repeatedly.

### 🚨 Severity Estimation

Estimates the severity of an issue to help authorities understand its urgency and potential impact.

### ⚡ Smart Prioritization

Helps prioritize urgent and high-impact civic problems for faster action.

### 📍 Location-Based Reporting

Associates reported civic issues with their locations for better identification and management.

### 👷 Field Worker Coordination

Supports assigning reported issues to appropriate field workers for resolution.

### 🔍 Issue Verification

Supports verification of reported issues before or during the resolution workflow.

### 📊 Management Dashboard

Provides authorities with a centralized view of reported and ongoing civic issues.

### 🔔 Status Tracking

Allows issue progress to be tracked through different stages of the resolution process.

## 🏗️ System Workflow

### 1️⃣ Report

A citizen submits information about an urban issue.

### 2️⃣ Detect

The system analyzes the submitted issue and identifies its characteristics.

### 3️⃣ Check for Duplicates

The system checks whether a similar complaint already exists.

### 4️⃣ Estimate Severity

The issue is evaluated to determine its potential severity.

### 5️⃣ Verify

The reported issue can be reviewed or verified before further action.

### 6️⃣ Prioritize

Issues are organized according to urgency and importance.

### 7️⃣ Assign

The appropriate field worker or responsible authority can be assigned.

### 8️⃣ Resolve

The field team works on resolving the reported issue.

### 9️⃣ Track

The issue status is updated throughout its lifecycle.
